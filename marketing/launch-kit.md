# SiteSignal launch kit

Use these as starting points. Post only where product feedback or small business tools are welcome, follow each community's self-promotion rules, disclose that you built SiteSignal, and answer replies yourself. Do not mass-post the same copy.

## Founder / product feedback post

**Title:** I built a free homepage audit that shows its evidence instead of inventing a revenue score

I made SiteSignal after getting tired of website “audits” that give a mysterious score and promise a conversion lift without showing what they measured.

Paste a public homepage and it checks the returned HTML for things like the main headline, contact path, mobile viewport, HTTPS, share preview, and a small sample of internal links. You get the evidence and a practical next step for each finding. It does not estimate lost sales.

It is early, so I would value feedback on whether the findings are useful and what the scan should check next. Try it: https://website-revenue-leak-scanner.pages.dev/

I built it, and I will be in the thread to answer questions.

## First-week loop

1. Run the scanner on your own site and fix any incorrect or confusing findings.
2. Share one honest build/learning post in one founder community that permits tool feedback.
3. Ask five people who run small businesses or build websites to try one scan; request specific feedback.
4. Improve the three most confusing findings before widening promotion.
5. Track visits, completed scans, exports, and paid review requests separately. Do not treat impressions as customers.
